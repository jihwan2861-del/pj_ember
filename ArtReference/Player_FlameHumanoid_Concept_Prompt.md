## 최종 프롬프트

Use case: style-transfer
Asset type: one transparent pixel-art flame character concept for a 2D platformer.
Input image 1: edit target, the humanoid pixel character. Input image 2: fire-shape, color and pixel-art treatment reference.
Primary request: transform ONLY the humanoid from image 1 into a living flame character, using image 2's simplified orange flame shapes, small cream-white bright core/eye shapes, red trailing flame accents and cream-white pixel outline.
Preserve image 1's overall height-to-width proportion, head/body size relationship, slightly right-facing stance, arm arrangement, and widely separated two-leg pose. Keep a recognizable humanoid torso, two arms and two legs, not a floating flame blob. Replace the cyan circular head with a compact orange flame head, with short flickering flame tips instead of hair. Make the head flame contour organic but chunky; do NOT enlarge the head into a huge mascot head. Two small bright cream pixel eye shapes directed slightly right. No mouth.
The green torso, pink arm and blue trousers become coherent warm orange/red flame body regions, with a modest yellow/cream heat core. Shape the fingertips and ends of feet as short soft flame points. A small red trailing flame accent around the waist echoes reference 2 without adding an actual fabric costume.
Style: extremely simple coarse pixel art, same large square pixel blocks and low detail as the references, as if a roughly 24x32 game sprite enlarged by nearest-neighbor. Flat limited palette, strong stair-step silhouette. Single restrained cream-white pixel outline echoing reference 2. No tiny texture, painterly fire, realism, gradients, antialiasing or blurry glow.
Output: ONE complete character only, centered with generous transparent margin, portrait composition. All original gray checkerboard squares, cursor mark, UI and black human outline removed. Genuine transparent alpha background, not a painted checkerboard.
No scenery, ground, shadow, text, labels, extra characters, sprite sheet, frames, watermark or border.
