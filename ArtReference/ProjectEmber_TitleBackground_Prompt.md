# Project Ember title background

- Mode: built-in image generation, new environment illustration with composition references.
- Destination: `Assets/image/ProjectEmber_TitleBackground.png`.
- UI/title/menu text is not baked into the image. It is editable in the Unity scene.
- References: the supplied Hollow Knight title screen (atmosphere and restraint) and Horizon title screen (landscape and asymmetric composition); neither logo is reused.

## Final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: background illustration only for the title screen of a 2D pixel platformer named Project Ember.
Input images: Image 1 is a reference for a restrained atmospheric title-menu mood only. Image 2 is a reference for a wide majestic landscape and asymmetrical negative space only. Do not copy their logos, text, interface, or distinctive structures.
Primary request: An original, beautiful 16:9 wide pixel-art landscape viewed from the shadowy entrance of an ancient blue stone ruin. Beyond a broken arch on the RIGHT, a luminous warm golden sun hangs above a distant misty valley and layered mountain silhouettes. Tiny warm embers rise near a modest flame-lit brazier on a stone ledge on the right. Moss, a few hanging vines, massive stone blocks, cool deep teal and navy shadows, distant blue atmospheric haze, soft restrained amber light.
Composition: The LEFT 45 percent must stay dark, quiet and low-detail so real editable Korean title and menu text can be overlaid there. The scenic focal point, arch, and warm fire are in the RIGHT half, with a clear sense of depth and an inviting horizon. The top-left and middle-left must not contain bright rays, fire, or busy objects. No character needed. The illustration fills the entire rectangle; no framing bars.
Style: Confident game-ready PIXEL ART, conspicuous square pixel clusters, stepped stone and mountain edges, limited cohesive palette, no smooth painterly rendering and no photographic textures. Evoke the atmospheric layered depth of a high-quality indie pixel platformer. Preserve the readability of chunky pixels even at large size.
Mood: Quiet anticipation, a small fire beginning an ambitious adventure toward the sun, warm light against cool ruins.
Output: one clean wide background image, ideally 1920x1080 landscape, opaque full-bleed.
Avoid: ALL text, letters, logos, titles, menu buttons, interface icons, captions, watermark, checkerboard, cartoon UI, giant flames, people, ornate logo ornaments, photorealism. This is environment artwork only, NOT a mockup of a menu.
