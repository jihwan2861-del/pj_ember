ART DIRECTION: simple charming doodles on near-white warm paper, thick imperfect black felt-tip outlines, loose visibly separate marker strokes, colored-pencil and crayon hatching, lots of unpainted paper inside forms. Paper texture should be subtle, mostly white. Avoid antique brown parchment, realistic rocks, 3D, pixel art, cinematic gradients, photorealism, and detailed painterly rendering.

Make its origin unmistakable.

PANEL 2 (upper right): the very same tiny glowing orange-yellow ember falls downward between a few fluffy outlined clouds and huge loosely drawn lavender mountain peaks.
